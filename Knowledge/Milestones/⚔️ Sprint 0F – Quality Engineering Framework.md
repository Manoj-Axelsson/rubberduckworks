# ⚔️ Sprint 0F – Quality Engineering Framework

## Mission

Establish Quality Engineering as a first-class engineering discipline within RubberDuckWorks by creating a permanent domain dedicated to engineering quality, continuous verification, and evidence-based software development.

---

## Objectives

- Introduce the Quality domain into the repository architecture.
- Define the structural foundation for Quality Engineering.
- Establish the philosophy that software quality is engineered rather than inspected.
- Prepare the repository for future documentation covering Test-Driven Development (TDD), Behavior-Driven Development (BDD), testing strategies, quality gates, tooling, and practical case studies.

---

---

## Engineering Significance

Quality Engineering is recognized as an engineering discipline equal in importance to Architecture, Governance, Methodology, and Standards.

Rather than treating testing as a final verification activity, the AXIS Engineering Framework considers quality to be a continuous engineering concern spanning the complete software lifecycle.

---

## Guiding Principle

> Engineering without evidence is engineering based on assumptions.

This principle establishes that confidence in software must be supported by measurable evidence rather than intuition or expectation.

Evidence may include:

- Architecture
- Specifications
- Automated tests
- Static analysis
- Code reviews
- Continuous Integration
- Observability
- Performance measurements
- Security verification
- Documentation

---

## Outcome

RubberDuckWorks now contains a dedicated Quality Engineering domain that provides the architectural foundation for future work on engineering philosophy, testing methodologies, quality gates, verification strategies, tooling, and real-world case studies.

This milestone completes the structural establishment of the Quality pillar within the AXIS Engineering Framework.
