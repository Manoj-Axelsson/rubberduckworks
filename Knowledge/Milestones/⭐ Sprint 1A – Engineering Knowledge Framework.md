# ⚔️ Sprint 1A – Engineering Decision & Knowledge Framework

**Status:** Completed

**Completion Date:** 2026-08-01

**Theme:** Engineering Knowledge & Decision Management

---

# Vision

Engineering is more than writing code.

Professional software engineering requires documenting the reasoning behind technical decisions, preserving lessons learned and creating a knowledge base that future contributors—including your future self—can rely upon.

Sprint 1A establishes that capability within RubberDuckWorks.

---

# Objective

Introduce a structured Engineering Knowledge Framework that captures engineering reasoning alongside implementation.

The aim is to evolve RubberDuckWorks from documenting projects into documenting engineering 
thinking.

---

# Why this Sprint was needed

Projects naturally accumulate knowledge over time.

Without structured documentation, important architectural decisions, engineering trade-offs and technical investigations are eventually lost.

This sprint introduces a permanent home for that knowledge.

---

# Deliverables

## Engineering Decisions

Created a dedicated knowledge domain.

```
Knowledge/
└── decisions/
```

Purpose:

- Preserve engineering reasoning
- Record important technical decisions
- Capture lessons learned
- Improve long-term maintainability

---

## Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

Introduced Architecture Decision Records.

Purpose:

- Explain architectural choices
- Record alternatives considered
- Document trade-offs
- Preserve architectural intent

---

## Engineering Case Studies (ECS)

Introduced Engineering Case Studies.

Unlike bug reports, Engineering Case Studies document:

- Engineering investigations
- Technical challenges
- Design decisions
- Root cause analysis
- Lessons learned

Initial publications include:

- ECS-001 – Race Condition in Checkout
- ECS-002 – Technical Debt Assessment
- ECS-003 – Code Review Philosophy

---

## Documentation Standards

Established:

- README structure
- ECS template
- ADR structure
- Navigation between engineering knowledge artifacts

---

# Repository Impact

Before Sprint 1A

RDW primarily answered:

> *What was built?*

After Sprint 1A

RDW also answers:

This marks a significant evolution from project documentation toward engineering knowledge management.
