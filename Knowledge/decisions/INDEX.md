# Engineering Decisions

Engineering Decisions preserve the reasoning behind important technical and architectural choices made throughout the RubberDuckWorks Engineering Laboratory.

Unlike implementation documentation, these records explain *why* decisions were made, what alternatives were considered, and what lessons were learned.
