# Engineering Case Studies (ECS)

Engineering Case Studies document practical engineering challenges encountered during software development.

Unlike bug reports, these documents focus on engineering thinking, investigation, trade-offs and lessons learned.

Every case study follows a structured approach.

Typical sections include:

- Executive Summary
- Background
- Problem Statement
- Investigation
- Engineering Decision
- Solution
- Validation
- Lessons Learned

Engineering Case Studies demonstrate how problems are analyzed, understood and solved using evidence rather than assumptions.

These documents complement the Architecture Decision Records by capturing engineering experience gained throughout the development lifecycle.
