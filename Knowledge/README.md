## Knowledge

***The collective memory of the Engineering Systems Laboratory.***

The Knowledge domain is where experience is transformed into reusable engineering understanding.

It exists to preserve observations, capture lessons learned, refine methodologies, and document patterns that improve future decision-making.

Software changes.

Knowledge compounds.

---

## Mission

The purpose of the Knowledge domain is to build an engineering knowledge base that is:

* Observable
* Searchable
* Reusable
* Continuously refined

Every document should contribute to a better understanding of systems, engineering practices, or decision-making.

---

## Philosophy

> **Reality is observed.
> Understanding is documented.
> Methodology is refined.
> Software emerges as a consequence—not as the starting point.**

Knowledge is considered a first-class engineering artifact.

---

## Structure

### Journal

The journal captures the day-to-day engineering journey.

Typical contents include:

* discoveries
* reflections
* experiments
* architectural decisions
* lessons learned

The journal values chronology and transparency over perfection.

---

### Methodology

Methodology documents ***how*** engineering work is performed.

Examples include:

* workflows
* design patterns
* validation techniques
* AI collaboration practices
* review processes
* continuous improvement methods

Methodology should evolve as new evidence becomes available.

---

### Documentation

Additional documentation may emerge over time:
- incident reports
- research notes
- architectural analyses
- decision records
- reference material

**Every document should have a clear purpose and an identifiable owner.**

---

## Knowledge Lifecycle

**Knowledge is never considered final. It is continously challenged, validated, and improved through engineeering practice.**

```text
Observation
      ↓
Reflection
      ↓
Documentation
      ↓
Pattern
      ↓
Methodology
      ↓
Reusable Knowledge
```
---

## Engineering Principles

#### 1. Evidence Before Opinion
- Claims should be supported by observation, experimentation, or experience.

#### 2. Continuous Refinement
- Documents are expected to evolve as understanding improves.

#### 3. Reusability
- Knowledge should help solve future problems rather than merely record past events.

#### 4. Accessibility
- Documentation should be understandable by future contributors, including future versions of ourselves.
---

## Relationship to the Laboratory

The Knowledge domain is informed by every other part of the Engineering Systems Laboratory.

* **Identity** defines who we are.
* **Governance** defines how we work.
* **Playground** generates experiments.
* **Knowledge** preserves what we learn.
