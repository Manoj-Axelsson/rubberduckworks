# North Star

> **We don't optimize for speed.**
>
> **We optimize for understanding.**

RubberDuckWorks aims to explore business systems engineering through disciplined experimentation, thoughtful documentation, and continuous learning.

Every decision should eventually make the laboratory:

    * easier to understand,
    * easier to maintain,
    * easier to contribute to,
    * and more valuable to the next engineer who enters it.

---

# Engineering Principles

* **Structure knowledge before structuring software.**
* **Model Reality Before Modeling Data and Writing Code.**
* **Understand first. Build second.**
* **Inspect before committing.**
* **Document before forgetting.**
* **Rebase instead of force pushing.**
* **Create incident reports.**
* **Maintain an engineering journal.**

---

# The RubberDuckWorks Method

Every idea follows the same path:

```text
Reality
    ↓
Understanding
    ↓
Documentation
    ↓
Models
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Data
    ↓
Code
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Improvement
```

Nothing skips a stage.

---

# Engineering Workflow

Every artifact progresses through increasing levels of maturity:

```text
Idea
    ↓
Playground
    ↓
Laboratory
    ↓
Documentation
    ↓
Domains
    ↓
Implementation
```

Ideas are explored before they are documented.

Knowledge is documented before it is modeled.

Models are validated before they become software.

Software exists to represent reality—not to define it.

---

# Our Commitment

RubberDuckWorks is more than a code repository.

It is an engineering laboratory where business systems are understood before they are implemented, where documentation is treated as a first-class artifact, and where every experiment is an opportunity to refine our understanding of how software should be built.

We believe that understanding compounds.

We document accordingly.

---

> **Every commit should leave the laboratory—and the next engineer's understanding of it—better than it was found.**
