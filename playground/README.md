## Playground

**Where ideas become experiments and experiments become knowledge.**

The Playground is the experimental environment of the **Engineering Systems Laboratory**. It is a safe space for exploring technologies, validating assumptions, testing methodologies, and learning through implementation.

Nothing here is expected to be production-ready.

---

### Purpose

The Playground exists to:

* Explore new technologies and frameworks
* Validate architectural ideas
* Test engineering methodologies
* Prototype solutions to real problems
* Learn by building and documenting

Every experiment should answer a question or validate a hypothesis.

---

### Philosophy

**Reality is observed.
 Understanding is documented.
 Methodology is refined.
 Software emerges as a consequence—not as the starting point.**

- Experiments are valuable regardless of their outcome.

- A failed experiment that produces understanding is considered a successful learning activity.

---

## Working Principles

#### 1. Validation First
- Do not assume.
- Build a small experiment, observe the result, and document the findings.

#### 2. Small and Focused

Each experiment should have a single objective and remain easy to understand.

#### 3. Documentation Over Memory

Record:
* Why the experiment exists
* What was tested
* What was observed
* What was learned
* What should happen next

#### 4. Disposable by Design

Experiments are temporary. Successful ideas may be promoted into the Knowledge domain or incorporated into production projects.

#### 5. Suggested Structure
```
playground/
├── experiment-name/
│   ├── README.md
│   ├── notes.md
│   ├── source/
│   └── findings.md
```
***Each experiment should be self-contained and reproducible.***

### 6. Experiment Lifecycle
```
Question
    ↓
Hypothesis
    ↓
Experiment
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Documentation
    ↓
Knowledge
```
***Knowledge gained in the Playground should eventually migrate into the appropriate location within the laboratory.***

### 7. Exit Criteria

An experiment leaves the Playground when it has produced one of the following:
* A validated engineering practice
* A reusable template
* A documented methodology
* A reference implementation
* A lesson learned
---
### Engineering Systems Laboratory
The Playground is not a sandbox for random code. It is a structured environment for disciplined experimentation, continuous improvement, and knowledge-driven engineering.
- **Build small.** 
- **Observe carefully.**
- **Document everything.**
