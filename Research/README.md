# Research

This directory contains research activities, investigations, literature reviews, and systematic studies conducted within RubberDuckWorks.

## Purpose

Research documents ground our understanding in evidence, best practices, and systematic investigation.

## Research Types

- **Literature Review** - Synthesis of existing knowledge
- **Case Study** - In-depth investigation of specific systems
- **Systematic Study** - Controlled investigation of patterns
- **Industry Analysis** - Understanding external trends and practices

## Research Process

1. **Question** - Define research inquiry
2. **Investigation** - Conduct systematic study
3. **Analysis** - Interpret findings
4. **Documentation** - Record methodology and results
5. **Integration** - Connect to laboratory knowledge

## Contributing

Research contributions should:

- Clearly state research questions
- Describe methodology transparently
- Present evidence and findings
- Discuss limitations and implications

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for guidelines.
